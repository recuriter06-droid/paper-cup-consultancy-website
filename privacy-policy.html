Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

PAPER CUP CONSULTANCY respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect through our website and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

When you contact us, apply for an opportunity, or submit information through our website, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Professional and employment information
  • Preferred location
  • Last CTC and notice period
  • Resume/CV and information contained in it
  • Any information you voluntarily provide in a message or enquiry

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information you provide to:

  • Review and process job applications
  • Match candidates with relevant employment opportunities
  • Contact candidates regarding suitable vacancies
  • Communicate with employers regarding recruitment requirements
  • Respond to enquiries and requests
  • Maintain recruitment and business records
  • Improve our recruitment services and website

3. Resume and Candidate Information

If you submit your CV or other career information, you authorize PAPER CUP CONSULTANCY to use that information for recruitment-related purposes, including considering you for suitable employment opportunities and sharing relevant candidate information with potential employers where appropriate for recruitment.

4. Sharing of Information

We may share relevant candidate information with prospective employers or other recruitment-related parties when necessary for recruitment purposes. We do not sell candidate information as a product or service.

We may also use third-party service providers for website hosting, email, recruitment management, form processing, document storage, or other business operations. Such services may process information on our behalf.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, misuse, loss, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention

We may retain candidate and enquiry information for as long as reasonably necessary for recruitment, business, legal, compliance, record-keeping, or dispute-resolution purposes.

7. Your Choices and Rights

You may contact us to request information about personal data we hold about you, request correction of inaccurate information, or raise a privacy-related concern. Where applicable, requests concerning consent and personal-data rights will be handled in accordance with applicable law.

8. Children

Our recruitment services are intended for individuals who are legally able to seek employment. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for recruitment purposes.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
